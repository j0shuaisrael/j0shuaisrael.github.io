Privacy Policy — Split

Effective 25 November 2026

Split is a split-screen web browser. This policy explains what Split does — and does not do — with your information. It is short, because there is not much to say.

What Split collects

Nothing. Split collects no personal data, no usage data, no analytics, and no crash reports. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server. Split does not send anything about you or your browsing anywhere.

What stays on your device

Your browsing history, cookies, and cached website data are stored only on your device, by Apple’s WebKit — the same engine Safari uses. Split keeps a list of sites you visited so you can return to them. You can delete it, along with cookies and cached data, at any time with Burn everything in Settings. Nothing is uploaded, synced, or shared.

Advertising

Split shows no ads inside the app, and uses no advertising or tracking SDKs. A support page on the web may carry advertising in the future; the app itself does not.

Websites you visit

When you browse with Split, the websites you visit may collect information about you according to their own privacy policies. Split does not control that, and cannot see what they collect. If a site asks for your location, camera, or microphone, iOS asks you first — Split never grants those permissions on your behalf.

Children

Split is not directed at children under 13, and collects no information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Because Split collects nothing, a change here can only ever narrow what the app does, not expand it.
